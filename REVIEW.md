5. Remaining advisories and limits
- review_queue(), review_submission(), and submit_indicators() remain authenticated-callable SECURITY DEFINER RPCs (still flagged by the Supabase Security Advisor, owner-reported 2026-10-29). Their definitions have pinned search paths, anon/PUBLIC execution is revoked, and each has an internal guard, but the guards differ: review_queue() and review_submission() require private.is_admin() (admin role plus AAL2/MFA); submit_indicators() requires a non-null auth.uid() and rejects anonymous sessions. Migration 10 also grants service_role EXECUTE. Do not conclude that grant is harmless solely because a service-role JWT would fail private.is_admin()—that reasoning applies to the reviewer RPCs, not directly to submit_indicators(). The effect of calling submit_indicators() with a service-role JWT has not been behaviourally verified and should be assessed explicitly. Keep the required authenticated grants unless a tested alternative preserves the app's intended behaviour; do not revoke them blindly. Next action: review each function's authorization checks and execution grants whenever they change, and revisit if the app moves to a non-public RPC architecture.
- Leaked-password protection is disabled (owner-reported 2026-10-29); it cannot be set with SQL. Next action: enable it in the Supabase Auth settings if the project's plan allows (not verified here); meanwhile enforce a long minimum password length, keep MFA mandatory for administrators and consider CAPTCHA on sign-up.
- Scan expiry is structurally verified at schema and scheduler level (2026-10-29, section 2) but its behaviour is unverified (no test database; section 8).
